Luka Modrić

Luka Modrić (born September 9, 1985) is a Croatian professional footballer who plays as a midfielder for the Croatia national team. In 2018, Modrić achieved a unique "Grand Slam" of football honors, sweeping the World Cup Golden Ball, the Ballon d'Or, and The Best FIFA Men's Player award, while also securing a third consecutive UEFA Champions League title. That same year, he successfully broke the decade-long duopoly held by Cristiano Ronaldo and Lionel Messi over the world's top individual football awards.
